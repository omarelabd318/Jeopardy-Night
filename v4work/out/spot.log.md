# spot (Most Spotify Listeners), new in v4.39
- 80 clues, 16 per value, generated by v4work/rank/gen.js. Each lists 3 artists; the answer gives all three monthly listener counts.
- Figures: Wikipedia "List of most-streamed artists on Spotify", monthly listeners table as of 2 October 2026; web-search figures for Imagine Dragons, Queen, Lil Wayne, Red Hot Chili Peppers, 50 Cent, Guns N' Roses, Metallica, Lewis Capaldi and the Arab artists (Sherine, Amr Diab, Elissa, Tamer Hosny, Mohamed Ramadan, Wegz, Marwan Pablo).
- Same safety margins as igf. Monthly listeners move every month, so re-run gen.js before a game night if it's been a while.
