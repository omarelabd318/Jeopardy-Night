## Notable fixes
- "Ya ma gab el ghorab": the black bird emoji (🐦‍⬛) is new, and older phones show it as a bird plus a black square. It's now 🐦⚫.
- "E'mel el kheir w armeeh fel bahr": 🙂➡️🌊 is now 😇🎁➡️🌊, so "doing good" is clearer.
- Made easier: Ramadan Kareem, El 'agala men el shaytan, Bokra fel meshmesh, Lesanak hosanak.
- New everyday phrases at 100 to 300 (Shokran, Ma'a salama, Ya nhar eswed, Ana fel sekka, Shobbeik lobbeik, Dammak khafeef, 'Ala fekra and more).
- New proverbs at 400 and 500 (Bab el naggar mekhalla', El hama 'a'raba, El eid el wahda ma tesa'afsh, El gamal ma yeshoofsh 'ewegt ra'abto, Kalam el leil madhoon bel zebda and more).
- None of the new proverbs repeat ones in Emoji Sentences.
