## Doubtful or edited

- **food-bigmac**: plain Commons burger photo (no box, wrapper or logo); a Big Mac look-alike rather than a branded shot. Padded to landscape on white.
- **food-fatta**: restaurant photo; a beer bottle label at the top was blurred.
- **food-hawawshi**: small corner watermark blurred.
- **food-churros**: served in paper cups with a tiny printed line on them (not readable at game size).
- **food-eggplant**: long Asian aubergines, not the round Egyptian kind.
- **food-galangal**: raw rhizomes, which look a lot like ginger. Fair for a 500 but expect ginger guesses.
- **food-pho**: cropped to the bowl to drop a restaurant sign and a drink can.
- **food-ceviche**: top edge trimmed to drop a small label.
- **food-salsify**: black and white salsify roots on a board; hard to tell from other roots, as expected at 500.
- Cut open as asked: dragon fruit, kiwano, mangosteen, pomegranate, durian, plus kiwi, avocado, persimmon, tamarillo, feijoa, cherimoya, kumquat, Jerusalem artichoke.

- **4.99 batch (20 more)**: food-corn padded on white (cut-out photo); food-ratatouille padded with a blurred copy of itself (portrait); food-kimchi is a six-bowl collage; food-salak has a small scale bar; food-breadfruit and food-fig padded to landscape.
