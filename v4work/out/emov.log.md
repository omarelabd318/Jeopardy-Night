## Notable fixes
- Removed the second "The Silence of the Lambs" clue (🐑🤫 at 500); the 🔪🐑🔇 one at 300 stays.
- "Wonka" and "Charlie and the Chocolate Factory" both use a top hat and chocolate, so each answer now accepts the other Willy Wonka films.
- "Sherlock Holmes" now also accepts the TV show "Sherlock". The Narnia clue now also accepts "Narnia".
- Seinfeld: the old emojis (🥣🗽😐) didn't point anywhere. They're now 🎤🗽🥣🚫 (stand-up, New York, "No soup for you!").
- Made easier: TMNT, It, Wednesday, Mr. Bean, The Crown, The Last of Us, 12 Angry Men, Shutter Island, Singin' in the Rain, El Feel El Azraq.
- Made harder: Happy Feet, The Princess and the Frog, Godzilla vs. Kong.
- The new clues mix family classics (Cars, Ice Age, Madagascar), big hits (Dune, John Wick, Barbie), classics (Casablanca, Psycho, Lawrence of Arabia) and TV (Lupin, Chernobyl, The Bear, Ozark).
- All emojis were checked against the Unicode emoji list (Unicode 14 or older), so they show up on older phones too.
- 500 has 34 clues, not 35, because one duplicate was removed. That's within the allowed difference of 2.
